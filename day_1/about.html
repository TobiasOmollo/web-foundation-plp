About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a note-taking tool designed to help you write down thoughts, tasks, and ideas the moment they strike.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Navigate to the Home page and locate the "Add a note" form.
  2. Type your thought into the text input field.
  3. Press Enter to save it to your list.

Key Features

  • Minimalist, distraction-free interface
  • Real-time counter tracking your total notes
  • Fast keyboard-accessible inputs for rapid capture
  • Lightweight semantic markup with zero bloat
